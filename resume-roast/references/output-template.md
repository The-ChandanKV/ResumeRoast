# Report template

Use the headings below in order. Replace instructional text with the actual assessment. When a section has no findings, briefly explain why.

```markdown
# RESUME ROAST REPORT

## 1. Overall resume assessment score
**NN/100.** This is a custom resume assessment score. It does not represent an employer's ATS score.

## 2. Hiring suitability summary
State the level of alignment evidenced by the resume, the strongest match, and the most material gap.

## 3. Score breakdown
| Category | Score | Justification |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Technical skills alignment | NN/30 | Specific evidence and deductions |
| Experience and project relevance | NN/25 | Specific evidence and deductions |
| Keyword alignment | NN/20 | Specific evidence and deductions |
| Resume structure and readability | NN/15 | Specific evidence and deductions |
| Quantifiable achievements | NN/10 | Specific evidence and deductions |
| **Total** | **NN/100** | Sum of the five scores |

## 4. Matching skills
Map the required and preferred matches to supporting resume evidence.

## 5. Missing skills
Identify required and preferred gaps, partial coverage, and any explicit eligibility conditions that the resume does not establish.

## 6. Critical resume mistakes
For each material issue, describe where it appears, why it matters, and how to correct it.

## 7. Weak bullet points and improved versions
Repeat this group for every weak bullet:

- **Original** — exact resume bullet
- **Problem** — specific weakness and why it matters
- **Improved** — evidence-backed rewrite, with a labeled placeholder if useful
- **Why it works** — what the reader can understand from the revision

## 8. Keyword recommendations
Give supported terms with suggested placement. Flag unsupported terms as conditional or as learning targets.

## 9. Formatting improvements
Give fixes for observable layout, organization, or readability issues. State any limits of inspection.

## 10. Top five priority actions
Rank up to five distinct fixes with the highest-impact action first.

## 11. Final assessment
**Resume quality** — assess the submitted document and what would improve it.

**Actual role eligibility** — identify what the supplied evidence establishes and what remains unverified.
```
